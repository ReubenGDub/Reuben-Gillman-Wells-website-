01 / ABOUT

Curious about how sounds can open new worlds.

I write music drawn to nature, change, and storytelling. My work moves between choral and orchestral writing, music for small ensembles, cinematic screen pieces, and various songwriting techniques.

I'm very interested in the way particular sounds can open up to a new world. This is a place to share the music I'm making, the ideas behind it, and the things I'm discovering through the journey.
