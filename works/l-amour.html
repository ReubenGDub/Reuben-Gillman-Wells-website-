L'amour

Program Notes

This piece is one that can be scaled from an intimate string quartet up to a full string ensemble of 40+ players. It is quite a versatile piece that can be played slow and beautiful, or lively and joyful to meet many contexts.

L'amour is French for the concept of love, and this piece reflects the journey of love through it's forms in a relationship. It moves through sparks and butterflies, to the tests and stretches of life, to the beautiful soaring love that is deep and powerful.

The melodic and harmonic material was closely inspired by Can't Help Falling in Love by Elvis Presley, the song that this piece was accompanied by in its premiere performance.

Instrumentation

Violin I, Violin II, Viola, Cello, (opt. Double Bass)
